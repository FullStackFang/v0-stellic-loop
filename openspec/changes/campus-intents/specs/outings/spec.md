## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Going on a proposal
"Going" on a proposal, from its card, its ask card, its ask email or its intent, SHALL put the person in that proposal's one outing for its intent, whoever else they know is in other outings on the same event. It SHALL NOT use the pick by people they know. After joining, the panel SHALL open on "Invite people". "I'm in" pressed on the same event outside the intent SHALL keep the pick by people they know.

#### Scenario: A friend in another outing
- **WHEN** a person in "Run the island" presses Going on the matched "Saturday 5K", and a friend of theirs is in a different outing on that 5K
- **THEN** they join the intent's outing on the 5K, not the friend's

#### Scenario: From the event itself
- **WHEN** a person not in the intent presses I'm in on the "Saturday 5K" listing
- **THEN** they join an outing by the usual pick, as before
