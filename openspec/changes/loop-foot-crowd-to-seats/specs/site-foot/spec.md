## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: The foot is the bar alone
The foot of a page SHALL be one bar holding the wordmark, Terms, Privacy, the copyright line and, where the page wears no colours of its own, the colour dots. It SHALL hold no ticket and no animals.

#### Scenario: A page with the foot
- **WHEN** This week, a groups page, Doodles, a legal page or the front door is open
- **THEN** its foot is the bar, with no ticket, no "Pick one up" and no "Admit everyone" above it

#### Scenario: The foot's links
- **WHEN** the foot is drawn
- **THEN** its links are Loopd, Terms and Privacy, in that order

#### Scenario: A page that wears its own colours
- **WHEN** the front door draws the foot in a school's colours
- **THEN** the bar wears those colours and has no colour dots
