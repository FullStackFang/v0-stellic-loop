Quorum · working name · throwaway mockup · v2

Post an intent. Tap in. When enough of you overlap on a day, it asks you to lock it.

No host, no number required. "Suits" is enough to post. Anyone can tap in; their free days come with them. Anyone in can add a link, a place, a note. Nobody picks a date: when enough people can do the same day, the board prompts "6 of 9 can do Tue. Lock it in?" and any of them can. That's the ON moment: texts go out, the invite lands in calendars. Until then nobody is on the hook.

  1. Board · every intent, nearest-to-prompt first. In / maybe / out. Tap a card for the details, the day heat, who's in, the share link.
  2. You · your free days for the window (or let your calendar mark them), your name visibility, what you're in.
  3. + · post an intent. Title only is fine; everything else is optional and can be added later, by anyone in.
Try: Board → Lock in Tue 16 on Suits in Hoi An (6 overlap, prompt showing) · tap I'm in on Overnight boat (your free days attach; conflict flag with Street-food on Wed) · open Street-food crawl → add a link in Details (attributed to you) · + → post "Night market" with just a title · You → mark Sun 14 free and watch the heat on the Board change.
Static click-through, state resets on reload. Design language = Loop (raised paper, facts-only cards). Names and numbers are made up. Window: trip week Feb 14–20, 2027. Default quorum when no number is set: 4 on the same day; intents with no day needed prompt at 4 in.
