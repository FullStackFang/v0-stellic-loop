Quorum · working name · throwaway mockup

Say what you want. Planners get a warm room. Things happen at a number.

Inverted planning. Instead of a host guessing and herding, everyone first says what they want and when they're free. Planners see the aggregate, never the individuals. A proposal posted from a want lands on the people who asked for it, and it only becomes real when enough of them tap in.

  1. You · tick the things you'd do, mark your free days (or let your calendar do it), choose whether your name shows.
  2. Plan · the warm room. Counts, best days, typical budget. One tap posts a proposal prefilled from the aggregate.
  3. Board · proposals with a minimum. In / maybe / out. Hit the number and it's ON: the yeses get a text, the poster gets the list.
Try: Board → tap I'm in on Suits in Hoi An (two short → the "one more" text) · Overnight boat shows a conflict flag because you're in for Street-food crawl that day · tap any card for the WhatsApp preview and share link · Plan → Post this on Cooking class · You → turn off "show my name", then look at the Board.
Static click-through, state resets on reload. Design language = Loop (raised paper, facts-only cards). Names and numbers are made up. Trip week Feb 14–20, 2027.
