Quorum · working name · throwaway mockup · v5

Post an intent. Tap in. When enough of you overlap on a day, it asks you to lock it.

No host, no number required. "Suits" is enough to post. Anyone can tap in; their free days come with them. Anyone in can add a link, a place, a note. Nobody picks a date: when enough people can do the same day, Quorum sends the group a message, "6 of 8 can do Tuesday. Lock it in?", and any of them can. That's the ON moment: texts go out, the invite lands in calendars. Until then nobody is on the hook.

  1. Board: every intent, nearest-to-prompt first. In / maybe / out. Tap a card for details, the day heat, who's in, the share link.
  2. You: your free days for the window (or let your calendar mark them), name visibility, what you're in.
  3. Post: pick an icon, type a title; everything else is optional and can be added later, by anyone in.
