Quorum · three ways to bring Delta in

Same board, same data. Delta's recipe: flat plates with no outline and no drop shadow, dusty pastels (coral, baby blue, granny smith, jasmine, lavender), Davy's grey instead of black, white glyphs, small corner radius, geometric type. Each frame takes more of it than the last. Under every phone: the whole icon set in that treatment. Pick one, or name the bits you want mixed.

1 · Delta iconsonly the plates and faces change

Smallest step. Delta's dusty palette on squarer plates with white glyphs, Delta pastels on the avatars. Everything else is the Mix untouched: Fraunces, carnelian seals, sun bubble, soft shadows.

2 · Delta palettegeometric UI, Fraunces kept for the mast

The middle. Delta's colours run through the system: coral for ready, cadet for on, jasmine instead of sun, Davy's grey for the dark fills. Card titles, numerals and labels move to Outfit (a Now-alike geometric sans). The one wonky serif line stays on the mast as the warmth.

3 · Delta all the wayflat, two-tone, geometric

Full commitment. No shadows at all; depth is a darker step of the same hue, the way Delta shades. Outfit everywhere, tracked caps wordmark, squared-off buttons, Davy's grey text. Reads like the icon pack became an app.
