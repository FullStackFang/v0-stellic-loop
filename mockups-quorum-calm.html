Quorum · the grown-up combination

Same board, same data. All three share one base: Luma's calm (Geist, ghost buttons with no border, hairlines, time first), aigma's serif for the one display moment, Delta only in the icons and kept small. No seals, no stickers, no speech bubbles, no wonky italics. They differ in which reference leads the structure. Pick one, or name the bits you want mixed.

0 · Nowthe Mix, for reference

Fraunces wonky italic, seals, bubble ask, 48px saturated plates.

A · Luma listrows, time first, one registration card

Luma leads. Intents are rows on hairlines, not cards: day on top in grey, title, faces and cost under. The count sits right like a price. Only the one that's ready gets a card, styled like Luma's "Registration" box with a grey band and a full-width button. Delta plate at 36px, true Delta colours.

B · aigma ledgernumbered sections, the count as a stat

aigma leads. "01 Open · 02 On" with dotted leaders and a № counter. Each card is a stat card: a big serif numeral in carnelian, "of 8 can do Tue" in italic serif, a tiny tracked tag. Dotted rule above the footer, go-circle on the right. Delta plates tinted into the paper with the glyph in the plate's darker shade.

C · HAI editorialdark hero, date blocks, pills with arrows

HAI leads. One ink hero block with one square corner carries the mast and two pill buttons. Every intent gets a date block (month, day) like HAI's events; the best day is the date. Cards keep the tucked corner. One lime chip for "ready", the way HAI tags news. Delta plates small next to the title.
