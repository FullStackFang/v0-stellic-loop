The page is a page of lecture notes. Ruled paper, the red margin line, punched holes. Each game is drawn in ink in the notes: Conga as a hand-drawn map of the real landmarks with your line walking it, Seats as a sketched grid of tiles, some turned over. The name stays on its tilted ink plate; under it, the campus line in the margin's red and three jotted notes. Play is a plain button.

Each game is a page of ink stamps, like a rubber stamp on a pass. Conga’s page is the real places it walks past, each a round stamp in outline; Seats’ page is the animals. Once you have played, the ones you met are stamped in colour, a little crooked, as a real stamp lands; the rest stay as dotted outlines waiting. Shown here with three of each stamped.
