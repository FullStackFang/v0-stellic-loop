Drop an intent · pill styling, 3 looks

Same composer as direction 01 (opens filled with the most popular recent drop, greyed = suggested; tap a word to make it yours; ↻ another rotates through what's popular; the count reacts live). Only the token treatment and the picker change. Flip theme to check both.

01

Soft pills · dropdown

recommended
  • Pills are tinted by category (eat amber / study slate / move moss) once they're yours; suggested pills are dashed grey outlines, so "yours vs. suggested" reads at a glance.
  • Chevron ▾ signals tappable without the dotted underline. Ranked dropdown menu is unchanged from the Floor sheet.
  • Quietest of the three; sits comfortably in the current sheet with no extra height.

Try: tap dinner → coffee · tap Grabbing → Up for · tap + where.

02

Bold pills · chip cloud

  • Filled, saturated pills — the sentence looks like a row of chips you'd see on a Floor card. Suggested = tinted + dashed.
  • Tapping a pill opens an inline chip cloud under the sentence (all options visible, ✦ marks popular, counts on each) instead of a floating list — more thumb-friendly, no menu-off-screen problems.
  • Trade-off: louder; four solid pills can compete with the Drop button.

Try: tap Thursdays → cloud → Fridays; tap + where → Collegetown.

03

Slot cards · rail

  • Each token is a mini card with a tiny label (doing / what / day / often / where) — no guessing what a slot means; "· suggested" lives in the label.
  • Picker is a horizontal rail of chips ranked by demand, so the sentence never gets covered.
  • Most explicit; reads a touch more like a form than a sentence.

Try: tap the often card → rail → 2× a week.
