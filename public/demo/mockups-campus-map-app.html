The map inside the campus page

What changes, and what does not

  1. The top is the live page's.The bar, the search line and the two lists are what /c/cornell has today, in its lime and navy. The map takes the page's colours, so a school's own colours would recolour it too.
  2. Near you starts close.It opens tight on you and the event that is on, and travels to the next one as you turn the dial. With nothing on it goes as close as the map goes. Pinch out to see the whole section, no further.
  3. The map has a wall.You can never drag or pinch past the whole-section view, so there is no empty ground to get lost in. The rim flashes when you hit it. Three buttons above the dial: recenter, zoom out, zoom in.
  4. Push the wall to go on.Drag until the map stops and the way on appears at that edge: the place in that direction, with an arrow. Tap it to go. A way that is not open yet is the same ticket greyed out: it shakes once and goes nowhere, and the map stays where you left it. Move back off the wall and it is gone.
  5. One new view: Near you.A third choice beside "Best for you" and "All of CampusGroups". It is the map with the dial, one section at a time.
  6. One search line for all three.In Near you it searches the section on screen and says what is next door.
  7. Post a plan stays in the bar.From here it pins the plan to where you stand.
  8. The lists are not fenced.They still show the whole campus week. Only Near you keeps to one step from where you stand.
  9. Made up for the mockup.The events, people and section edges. The live Cornell page has no events this week.
