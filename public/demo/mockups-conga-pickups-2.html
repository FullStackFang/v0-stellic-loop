Conga · pickups, round two

New drawings for the megaphone, the coffee and the whistle, and three ways to show how far the megaphone reaches. Pick an icon set and it is used in the scenes below.

The icons

1 · Linocut

Drawn like the trees and halls already on the map: heavy ink line, flat colour, one light patch, a hard shadow down and right.

2 · Toy

Drawn like the animals: no ink line, a deeper edge of the same colour, a glossy highlight, and a soft shadow on the ground under it.

3 · Badge

An enamel pin: the pickup in ink on a coloured coin with a paper ring. The simplest shape, so it reads best when small or far off.

How far the megaphone reaches

A · Puffy edge

The ground inside the reach goes a shade lighter, edged with the same puffy foam as the water's shore, turning slowly. Strays inside hop toward you.

B · Shout

Rings go out from the megaphone's mouth and stop at the edge of its reach, where a soft dotted line stays. The reach is shown by the shouting itself.

C · Lasso

A loop of your own rope, in your line's colour, thrown round the reach and turning slowly, with a knot where it closes. Every line's megaphone is told apart by its colour.
