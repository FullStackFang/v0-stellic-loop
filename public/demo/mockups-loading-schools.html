Things to know. Most waits are under two seconds, so whatever sits beside the ticket has to read in the first frame; every direction starts already filled in and the motion is only a bonus for a long wait. The names and counts would come from "Sign up my school", counted per school, with only schools that a few people asked for. The same names would show for every school's campus, the school you are at is never in the list. The small loader on buttons stays as it is.
