Loop · intent → anchor → loop

How a forming anchor reads next to a real loop

Same flow on both phones: your intent is personal, it lands in a shared anchor others can add a yes to, and at quorum the anchor fires and everyone who said yes is asked to join. No Join button exists before the fire. The two directions differ only in whether an anchor borrows the loop card's shape or gets its own.

A One card family

Anchor is a stripped loop card

Forming and real share the same card silhouette. An anchor is dashed, no faces, a meter and "N of M want it", and its only action is I want this too. A real loop has a rhythm line, faces, a streak, and Join loop. Waiting reuses the same anchor card.

B Two shapes

Anchor is a count tile

Anchors are compact tiles with a ring gauge, two to a row, under "Wanted near you". Real loops are full rows with faces under "Meeting near you". The shapes never overlap, so a count can't be mistaken for a crew. Waiting is a list of ring rows.
