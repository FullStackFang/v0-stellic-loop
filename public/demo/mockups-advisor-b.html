Scheme B, restyled three ways

Check on · Route · Pulse stays. What changes is how components separate. The old version put a gauge, a red mono line, a number column and a chevron on every row, so nothing had an edge. Each direction below picks one way to draw the edge and removes the rest. All open on Route.

B1 · ledger

Space and type do the separating

No lines between rows, no gauges, no chips. One ink. A small-caps label with a single hairline opens each group; the number column is the spine. Colour appears twice: the action, and a falling delta. Tap a row and the reason unfolds in prose.

try: tap Jameson · tap Nudge · switch to Pulse and watch the line draw
B2 · trays

Each group sits in its own tray

A group is a page-tinted tray with its title inside. Rows are hairlined within the tray, never outside it, so the tray edge is the only container on the screen. The action lives in the fold, with who it goes to beside it.

try: tap ECON 1110 · tap Flag to staff · tap Check on
B3 · cards

Routed spots are cards; everything else is a row

The three things she can act on are physical cards with a band in their unit colour, one open at a time, lifting off the paper when open. Watch, people and the Pulse breakdown are plain rows. The card shape means "act here", nowhere else.

try: tap Transfers, then Jameson · send one · tap Pulse
