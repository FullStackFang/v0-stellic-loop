Conga · the lighthouse light

Round three: no beam. The lamp turns, and where its light passes, sparkles pop up and fade behind it, sweeping round the island like a clock hand. Each frame is the real island from the game.

A · Gold dust

Lots of small warm-gold sparkles, thick near the lamp and thinning out, each twinkling for about a second after the sweep passes.

B · Bright glints

Fewer, bigger white four-point stars that pop in as the sweep reaches them and shrink away, reaching right out over the river.

C · Rising sparks

Gold sparkles that pop up where the sweep passes, then float upward a little as they fade, like sparks off the lamp.
