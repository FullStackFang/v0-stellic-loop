From an area to a spot

A plan can start loose: "dumplings tonight after class @downtown". The @place is the area. The exact spot is a second, optional thing that the people going settle between them, on the plan itself. Nobody has to fill it in to post, and nobody has to approve it.

You are one of four people going. Open the plan and you see the spots people have suggested in @downtown, side by side. Tap "I'm for this" on every spot you would be happy with (the button then reads "You're for this"; tap it again to take it back. The suggestion stays, at 0, for others to back. If you suggested it and nobody is for it, an x removes it), or suggest another: the box always offers two or three places in the area that nobody has suggested yet, so one tap is enough and it is clear what to type. The one with the most people is what the ticket shows. Once more than half of those going back the same spot, it is the spot, and the map appears. Each spot Loop can place has a small map tile in its corner. Tap the tile and the map takes the whole panel, in the Print look, with every suggested spot as a stamp (the one you opened is the coral one; tap another stamp to move to it). The x in the corner, or Esc, goes back to the spots. Try it: back Dumpling House.
