The space, your week, and the one that didn't form.

Chrome is paper and ink. Every plan row is a block in its own light: the hour and the forecast of that plan, with the same hard shadow swinging the way its sun would. The list is never one color. The didn't-form page is what happens when the sky goes out: flat grey, no sun, the count stays honest.
