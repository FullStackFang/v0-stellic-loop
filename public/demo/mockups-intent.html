Drop an intent · 3 directions

Today the FAB opens a blank textarea (ComposeSheet), and onboarding + the You tab each have their own free-text box. All three directions below replace those with one guided composer that opens already filled with the most popular recent drop on your floor — greyed as a suggestion — so an intent is never a blank page and always lands in the vocabulary The Floor already speaks (Grabbing dinner Thursdays, weekly). Every token is tappable, every menu is ranked by how many people want it. Tap ↻ another to pipe a different popular drop in.

  • Zero-blank start. Opens with the hottest recent drop, all tokens greyed. Tap ↻ to rotate through what's popular; tap a token to change just that word — it turns solid (yours), the rest stay grey.

  • Trailing + where slot is optional; leaving it open keeps "N near you" high — same open-to-all logic as the Floor sheet.
