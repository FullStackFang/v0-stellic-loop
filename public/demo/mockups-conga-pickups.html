Conga · pickups

Three ways the megaphone, the coffee and the whistle could look. What each one does is the same in all three. What changes is how it sits on the ground, how you grab it, and where its time runs down. Each scene runs a pickup for about five seconds; in the game they last longer.

Megaphone · strays come from twice as far, 10 s
Coffee · dash without dropping anyone, 8 s
Whistle · the bus and tram can't take anyone, 15 s
A · Stickers

A round paper sticker with the pickup on it, bobbing on the ground among the strays. Grab it and it flies up beside your name, where a red ring runs down. Plain and easy to spot from far off.

B · Carried in

A stray walks round holding it. Gather that stray and your own animal takes it: it holds the megaphone out front, carries the cup, wears the whistle. Your name tag is the timer, draining as it runs out. Nothing new on screen but the thing itself.

C · Campus stands

Fixed spots on the map, like the bus stop: a coffee cart, a soapbox, a ref's post. Walk through the circle to take one; the stand shuts for a while after. The time left shows up top beside your count. Fewer of them, and they become places lines race to.
