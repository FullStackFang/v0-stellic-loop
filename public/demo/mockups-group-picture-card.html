Group picture card: pictures first

Pressing the pencil opens the group's pictures, big. Press one to wear it. The gold + tile is the Ink+ part: it opens the animals to draw a new one. Everything here is clickable.

1Press the pencil: the group's pictures

The pictures are the card. The one the group wears is ringed; pressing another wears that one instead, and the tile above changes with it.

2Press +: the animals

Only after + do the animals show. Pick two to six, then "Draw us together" goes on to the Ink+ plate, the ask-first step and the two drawings, as built.
