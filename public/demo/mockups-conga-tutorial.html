Conga · teaching a new player

Today a new player on a phone gets one line at the top, "Drag to steer · double tap and hold to dash", for two seconds, so the dash is easy to miss. Three ways to teach it. Each shows only in a player's first game (and again from a small "How to play" in the start card); on a laptop the same steps say Mouse and Space.

Keep holding to keep going. Dashing drops an animal now and then.

The game plays on under it. Each card waits until you have done the thing (steered, picked one up, dashed), ticks, then slides to the next; a ghost finger shows the move. Three steps and you are playing, about 20 seconds. Skip ends it, and it never comes back on its own.

Three little moving pictures in the start card, read before you play. Quick, and it warns about running into other lines too, but you read it instead of doing it, so the dash can still be forgotten once the game starts.

C · A ghost hand
In the game
tap, tap, hold

No cards. Steering needs no teaching (you touch the screen and the line moves), so only the dash is shown: a ghost finger beside your thumb does tap, tap, hold every few seconds until you have dashed once. The least in the way, but it does not explain strays or running into lines.
