A spill, and drawing it in

Why it stays cheap

In the game a mob is one thing, not forty strays: one distance check per head each frame, and never more than sixteen of its animals drawn, whatever its count. A spill of 200 costs the same as a spill of 20. Left alone for twelve seconds it starts breaking up into ordinary strays, three a second, so mobs do not pile up.
