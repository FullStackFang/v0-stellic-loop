Admin animals, no tall card

Three ways to drop the 408px card on /c/admin → Animals. Each one works: drag, pick, type, press. Same animals in each, as they stand on Columbia.

AColumns

The grid is the four columns. Where an animal stands is the column it is in, so there is no "Move to" row at all. Drag a face to another column, or pick some and press a column's name. The one line on top edits the picked animal's name and words. Try: drag the fox into School.

BThe ticket

The grid stays; the card becomes a ticket along the bottom, 108px tall, full width. The face sits on the stub; name and words on the first line; where it stands is one sliding rail under them, with Save at the end. The face flies from the grid onto the stub. Try: pick the fox, slide it to School.

COpens in the grid

No card beside the grid. The picked animal opens where it is, five tiles wide and two tall, and the others step aside. Big face, name, words, the same rail, Save. Pick another and the first folds back. Try: pick the fox, then the panda.
