The campus page, and what it opens into

Three ways the first screen could work. Pick one, or parts of each. The four screens underneath are the same whichever you pick.

1. The first screen

The same campus, the same Tuesday, the same data. What differs is what a student meets first.

A · This weekA dated list of everything, like Luma. Feeds, posted plans and offers share one list. Rewards are a tag on the row. Tap an interest to filter.
B · Go togetherRewards lead. The offers open right now are the first thing seen, with what each group size earns. The list of things to do sits under them.
C · What do you want to do?Posting leads. You say what you want; Loop shows who is already planning it and which places reward it. The list is one tap away.

2. What it opens into

These work: tap the buttons.

An event from a feedPulled from CampusGroups. "I'm in" gives you a link for your chat. The organizer's reward is theirs to set.
Your outingWhat friends land on from the link. Everyone checks in with one button; the reward shows when enough have.
Post a planChange the interest, time or size and the places that reward it change with them. Picking one is optional.
A venue's viewCounts by week and by time slot. Offers switch on and off. No student names anywhere.
