How many animals you can make


The "Create your own" tile in the animal picker shows what is left: 3, 2, 1 on Ink, the Ink+ mark on Ink+. It drops only when you press Keep it. Three looks below, each in every state. Then the drawing screen with the draft.

ACorner badge

A small ink circle on the tile's corner, like the numbers on the group picture's animals. At 0 it turns pink. On Ink+ the circle holds the Ink+ mark.

BInside the tile

The plus gets smaller and "2 left" sits under it, inside the circle. Reads without knowing what a badge means. On Ink+ the plus is the Ink+ mark itself.

CThree dots

A little pill under the tile with one dot per animal, gold while unused. You see what you have used as well as what is left. On Ink+ the pill holds the Ink+ mark.

DThe drawing screen: a draft until kept

Same for every look. The new drawing is marked Draft. Your face and your count do not change until Keep it. Draw again replaces the draft and costs nothing.
