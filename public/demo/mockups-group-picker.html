Where a group livesThree ways to make the choice obvious. Each opens in its open state and is clickable.The approved form

1 · Path bar each step is a button

The place reads as a path of pills. Click any step to swap it for a group beside it; the dashed pill at the end goes one level deeper. Nothing to learn: every part you can change looks like a button.

2 · Field and tree one control, a proper list behind it

A single field with a label, the place and a chevron, so it reads as a control. Behind it the whole tree: each group with its mark, handle and size, folders that open and close, a search box, and a clear tag on the row the new group goes into.

3 · Columns walk down a level at a time

One column per level, like a file browser. Click a group to see what is inside it; the last one you clicked is where the new group goes. Best when the tree gets deep or wide.
