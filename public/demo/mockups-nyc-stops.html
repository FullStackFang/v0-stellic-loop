NYC campus: two things to pick

The map works the way Ithaca's does: one section at a time, and pushing against the edge shows the way on. In New York the way on is a stop. Pick how that sign looks, then pick how a cornell.edu person chooses a campus.

1. The way on, at the edge of the map

One stop is one section, so each map below is all of Roosevelt Island, pushed against an edge. Change the edge here and all three follow. A stop with nothing on is greyed and does not open.

Pushed against

AStation sign

The black plate over a platform: white rule, line colours, plain name. Reads as the subway at a glance. It is the one thing on the sheet that is not printed in the two inks.

BPrinted stop

Today's card, kept in the print look, with a strip of the line under the name: the stop you are at, the stop you are going to. Closest to Ithaca's arrow.

CTile tablet

The mosaic name set into a station wall. The most New York of the three, and the least plain: the name is in capitals and the line sits underneath.
