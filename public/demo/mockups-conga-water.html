2 · What a spill leaves

In all three: a scattered line of six or more leaves one of these where it fell, with its count. Walk your head into it and the whole count joins your line at once, growing from the back, with "+30" by your name. Nothing flies across the screen. Left alone it fades after twenty seconds and its animals come back as ordinary strays, a few at a time.
