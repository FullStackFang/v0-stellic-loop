The pickups as toys in the animals' own style, floating over the ground so they stand out, and the megaphone shown as it happens: walk over it, the view pulls back, and its reach opens round you.

The megaphone, as it happens

Your line walks to a megaphone lying ahead and takes it. The view pulls back, a smooth ring opens out from you to show how far it now reaches, and the strays inside come running. When its time runs out the ring closes and the view comes back in. Then another one turns up ahead, and it plays again.

The pickups on the ground

Each one floats a little over its shadow, rising and settling, and every second or so a soft paper ring goes out from it across the ground, so it catches the eye among the animals.
