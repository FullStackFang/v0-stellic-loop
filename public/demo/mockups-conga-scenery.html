Trees, cherry trees, the ferry, the tug, the cabs on the bridge, McGraw Tower and the lighthouse, made as toys in the animals' style. Halls stay as they are: each is the real building's footprint, so no two are the same shape.

What it costs to load and draw

All seven are one picture file of 36 KB, packed the way the animals are, so one extra download. The game can start on the ground as it is now and the toys appear a moment later. Trees, cherry trees and the two towers never move, so the game paints them onto the ground once and reuses that every frame, which costs about what the drawn ones cost now. Only the boats and cabs are drawn fresh, a handful at a time.
