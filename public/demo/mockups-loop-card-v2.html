Researcher read

The card is a presence instrument, not a page. Its job changes with the clock: before the meet it answers "when and where", during it answers "who is there yet", after it answers "did it happen, when is the next". The crew's state (here, on my way, not yet) is the connection signal; it replaces both chat and a going/declined grid.
