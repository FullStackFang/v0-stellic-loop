Ink+ in play, second round

Four different kinds of idea, each on the same line of animals led by the dachshund. They all move; give each a few seconds.

H · Paw prints

Your pet walks in ink: a trail of little paw prints, left and right, that fades behind it.

I · A splash when one joins

Nothing is on you while you walk. When an animal joins your line, it lands with a splash of ink.

J · A shine

Every few seconds a glint of light slides across your pet, like a foil card turned in the hand.

K · A braided rope

Your rope is braided in ink and red, and the braid runs along it as you go. Everyone else has the plain rope.
