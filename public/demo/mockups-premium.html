Questions.

Are free stamps really deleted?

Yes, after 30 days: the place, the time and the plan. Only the count stays, so levels and rewards still add up.

What if I stop paying?

The book stays for 30 days. Then old stamps go, as for anyone. Animals you were given or made stay yours.

What does my assistant see?

Your own stamps, your groups and this week on campus. Never who else was there. Off in one tap.
