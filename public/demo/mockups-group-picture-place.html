Where the group picture lives

Three places it could go, each shown open on Extreme Cheapskates. The card itself is the same in all three and follows your own-animal picker: round tiles, the gold ring for Ink, then the dark Ink plate with its ticket stub when someone without Ink+ presses the button. In A, press the pills under the card to walk through its steps.

A · On the group's own tile my pick

Press the group's tile, the card drops from it: the same move as pressing your own face in the bar. Only people who run the group get the pencil. Nothing new to find, no new tab.

B · While starting the group

A "Picture" row in the new-group form, so the group is born with its picture. Good for setting a group up in advance, but a group that already exists has nowhere to change it, so this would need A or C as well.

C · Its own "Picture" tab

A tab beside Members and Resources, with room for every past drawing. The most space, but it is a whole tab for something done once or twice, and members who cannot change the picture would see a tab with nothing to do.
