Loop · tap a row in Your week

What opens when you tap Lift Fridays

Three shapes for the loop card. Each phone starts on Home. Tap the Lift Fridays row, then poke the card: hype it, post to the crew, close it. Same content in all three; only the container changes.

Q1 · glance
Am I going, and when?

The next meet, the countdown, and my own state (in, hyped, on my calendar). Answered before anything scrolls.

Q2 · people
Who else is in?

The same four faces, named. A crew is a small fixed set, so show every member, not a stack with a +3.

Q3 · change
What did I miss?

Short posts from the crew and from Loop itself. Announcements, not chat. Newest first, three visible, no threads.

Guardrails kept: no going / declined grid · no stranger DMs (posts live inside a loop that already meets) · "can't this week" stays private · share, leave, report live behind "···"
A Sheet

The row lifts into a sheet

Home stays underneath. The card rises from the row you tapped, the title morphs into place, and a drag on the handle drops it back. Best for the glance-and-go case.

try: tap the row · drag the handle down · hype it · post
B Pass

A pass you could screenshot

One artifact per loop: your standing ticket. Top half is the meet, bottom half is the crew stubs. The board sits under the pass. Best for identity and sharing.

try: tap the row · move the pointer over the pass · post
C Board

The crew's board, summary pinned

The loop is its feed. A compact summary stays pinned and shrinks as you scroll; posts from people and from Loop fill the page. Best if the board becomes the daily reason to open a loop.

try: tap the row · scroll the board · react 🔥 · post from the dock

Researcher read

All three answer the three questions above the fold. They differ in what the loop is to a member: a moment (A), a membership (B), or a place (C).
