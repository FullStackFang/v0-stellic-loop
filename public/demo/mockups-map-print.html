Print, made finer

Navy and lime draw the sheet and stay quiet. One hot ink is kept for plans and nothing else, so a plan is the loudest thing on the page. Tap the times, tap a stamp, drag the map.

  1. Every plan is a stamp.Perforated edge, an engraved picture of what it is, and the hour. Friends ride along beside it.
  2. Picking one postmarks it.It grows, a lime burst opens behind it, a postmark lands, and the lime ink slides into register across the whole sheet.
  3. A ticket, with a stub.The plan hands you a ticket. The stub is the walk in minutes.
  4. Your walk is drawn on.A dotted line runs from you to the plan each time you turn the dial.
  5. The margins name next door.Like a real map sheet, the edge says where the next sheet goes. A scale bar measures in minutes of walking.
  6. Look around and the ticket lets go.Zoom out or drag away to see other plans and the ticket shrinks and fades. Tap any stamp or time to deal it again.
  7. The compass takes you home.It is the one button on the map: back to where you are.
