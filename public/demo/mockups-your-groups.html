Your groups, on the campus screen

Where a person finds every group they are part of: teams and clubs they belong to, groups inside those, and the outings and impromptu groups they are going to this week. Three places it could live. Each window is the same person, on the same Wednesday, in the same six groups. Everything is clickable. Pick one, or say what to mix.

ANames in the top bar, and a list at the foot of "On you" what is built now

Top-level groups are pills in the bar. Everything else (groups inside a group, and what you are going to) is at the bottom of the "On you" panel, shown open here. Cheap, but the bar runs out of room after three or four groups, and the full list is hidden behind a button named for something else.

BA "Groups" counter beside "On you"

One pill in the bar, with how many groups you are in. It opens a panel of its own: each group is a row with its kind, who is in it, what is next, and how many things in it are on you. Groups inside a group sit under their parent. What you are going to is the same kind of row. Start a group lives here too. "On you" goes back to being only the list of things to do.

C"Yours" on the filter line: your groups are tickets

No new panel. A third filter beside All and Near you turns the week's row into your groups, each one a ticket like every other ticket on this screen: the name, the kind, who is in it, what is next, and Open. An outing you joined is already a ticket, so it simply stays. Four whole tickets show at a time and the row scrolls to the rest. Shown with "Yours" pressed; press All to see the ordinary week.
