Conga · pond and tokens

The clay pond with its little things made to match the toys, and the sparkle and the clay token joined into one thing a spill leaves, played three ways.

1 · The clay pond, close up

Drawn in code, so free: lily pads as soft clay with a lighter top, a notch and a pink flower; reeds and cattails at the edge; two ducks paddling, each leaving soft rings; round stones at the shore; the ferry on the river. Drawn this way the pond costs about what it does now.

If you would rather have them as made toys like the buildings, these four pictures would do it, about 14 cents: a lily pad with a flower, a duck, a clump of reeds, a small rowboat. I will not make them unless you say yes.

2 · What a spill leaves

The token in all three: a clay coin in the fallen line's colour with its head animal on the face and its count on a tag, bobbing over its shadow, with sparkles turning round it. Walking into one adds its count to your line at once, growing from the back, with "+N" by your name; its sparkles shoot into your head. Tokens nobody takes fade after twenty seconds and come back as ordinary strays.
