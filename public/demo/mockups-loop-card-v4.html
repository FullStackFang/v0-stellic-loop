Which meaning should the color carry?

Both schemes use hues already in the palette (rose, slate, moss, amber) plus one new violet for music. The band tint is 13% of the hue over the surface, so it stays paper-like and never fights the carnelian accent.

What a glance tells youWhat kind of thing this is. Four different colors in a week means a varied week; a wall of slate means all study.Which world it came from: your floor, your class, campus. Tells you who these people are to you.

Recommendation: A, by activity, with the unit kept as text in the kicker.

Activity is the thing a student decides on ("do I want to go lift, or eat, or grind"), so it earns the color. The unit is context, and the kicker already says it. If study loops dominate a given week, slate cards still read as one calm block, which is honest.
