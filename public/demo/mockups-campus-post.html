Posting on the campus: three directions

None of these is a form. Each uses @places that people define themselves, and each shows what others close by are doing while you post. A pasted Luma or Posh link works in all three: it turns into the same thing a typed line does. Everything below is clickable. Pick one, or name the parts to mix.

AOne line

You type it the way you would text it. Loop reads the time and the @place out of the sentence and shows what it understood. Typing @ lists the places people near you already use.
