Prototype · ranking personalization

Same seeded run. Three people. Nothing on screen explains itself.

Josh seeds a Beebe Lake run on Donlon 2F. Sarah lives on that floor; Maya lives across campus. All three see the same world. Only the order, the walk time, and the state pill change.

Rule for this prototype: no card says “recommended because…”, “near your floor”, or “staff seeded”. Ranking is felt through ordinary product cues: where a card sits in the feed, the walk time already printed on it, and how much lift the top pick gets. Every word of explanation lives out here in the captions, never inside the phone.
