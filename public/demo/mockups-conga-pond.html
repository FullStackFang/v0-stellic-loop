Conga · pond and spill toys

The five new pictures (about 18 cents, as agreed), shown on the ground beside a hall and trees so you can judge their size and fit.

1 · Pond as a toy

A · Round pond (middle) and B · Long pond with jetty (right), each standing on the ground like a hall with its name under it. Lines walk round it and pass behind it; nothing moves on it. The river on the left stays the clay water you liked. Pick A or B for Beebe Lake.

2 · What a spill leaves

A · Sack, B · Medal, C · Popper, each left by a different line. The picture is the same for everyone; the number on it is drawn by the game, in the fallen line's colour, and shows how many it holds. Your line walks through them: each one pops, and its number joins your line with "+N" by your name. One icon per spill, nothing flying. Pick one.
