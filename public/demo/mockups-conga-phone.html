Conga · on a phone

Today a phone steers with one thumb and dashes with a big button under the other, and the scoreboard and controls sit over a third of the screen. Each phone below is the real game at phone size, playing itself.

1 · Dashing without a second button

A · Push past the ring

One thumb does both. Drag to steer as today; push your thumb out past the ring and the ring goes red and you dash. Ease back inside to stop. No button at all.

B · Second finger anywhere

Steer with one thumb; while any other finger is down anywhere on the screen, you dash. No button to find or aim at.

C · Double tap and hold

Tap twice quickly and keep holding the second tap: you dash while you steer with that same thumb. Lift to stop.

The Dash button bottom right, so both hands are needed, and the scoreboard and controls cover the top right.

2 · The scoreboard and controls

A · Your place beside your count

Gone while you play. Your place sits beside your line's count at the top. The controls show once, as one line when the game starts, then fade.

B · Tucked into a chip

A small chip top right with your place. Tap it and the top four open under it; tap again to close. The controls show once at the start, then fade.
