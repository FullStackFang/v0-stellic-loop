Ink+ in play

Three small ways an Ink+ line could look different while it moves. No blot under the head. The dachshund's line has Ink+; the panda's line beside it does not, so you can see the difference.

E · Sparks

The four-point spark from the Ink mark pops beside your pet now and then, and fades. Nothing stays on the ground.

F · Ink drops

Your pet leaves small drops of ink where it has walked. They dry and are gone in a few seconds.

G · Ink down the rope

Every few seconds a bead of ink runs down your rope from your pet to the last animal. The rope is otherwise the plain one.
