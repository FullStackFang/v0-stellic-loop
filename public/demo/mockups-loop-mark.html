The Loop mark, and the signed-out page as A + C

Four marks to replace the three overlapping circles, which read as a Venn diagram and are common as logos. Each is shown at its real sizes: 16px favicon, 22px in the rail, 28px, and large on the teal plate. Click a mark, or use the switcher further down, to see it as the art on the merged sign-in page.

All four keep the same idea: three people, one loop. They differ in how literal that is.

The teal plate fills the page. The mark draws itself in and then turns very slowly; its three parts are the three steps, and step 1 is lit lime and pulses. The steps then rise in beside it, ending on the lime Sign in pill. Replay to see the entrance again.
