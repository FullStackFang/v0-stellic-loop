How the map behaves

The rules in this mockup

  1. One section on screen, in its own shape.A section is a few blocks: what you would reach in one stop. The map fills the phone with it and veils the rest.
  2. One step from where you stand.You can step into the sections touching yours, and back. Tap a signpost, or the veiled ground beyond the edge. Signposts further out are shut until you walk closer.
  3. The map is the list.Every event is a pin. The sheet is only a rail of times: drag along it and the event speaks from its pin. Tap the time that is on to open its ticket.
  4. Search stays in the section.Try "run" in Collegetown: it says what is next door, and that more is further out.
  5. You post from where you stand.Next door you can look and join, not post.
  6. Tap a pin and it becomes a ticket.The map walks to it. In the demo, people you invite say yes after a second.
Streets and buildings: © OpenStreetMap contributors, tiles by OpenFreeMap. Section edges are drawn by hand and the events are made up.
