How the map behaves

The rules in this mockup

  1. One stop on screen.The map is a plate about four minutes' walk across. Nothing outside it is drawn.
  2. One stop either way.You can open the stop you stand in and the next one each way along the line. The rest stay shut until you move.
  3. Search stays inside the plate.Try "run" in Collegetown: it says what is next door, and that more is further along.
  4. You post from where you stand.Next door you can look and join, not post.
  5. Your people still show on the line.Faces sit over any stop where someone you know is in, even a shut one. This one is my addition.
  6. Tap a pin and it becomes a ticket.The map walks to it, and the sheet shows who is in and what it unlocks. In the demo, people you invite say yes after a second.

Three looks, same map

  1. A · Cutout.Coral ground, paper buildings with a hard shadow. Closest to the rest of Loop Campus.
  2. B · Blocks.Tilted, with coral blocks on a dark ground. The nearest to a 3D campus map, without the realism.
  3. C · Line.Streets only, drawn like a transit diagram.
