Conga · picking your animal

Today your seat opens a dark card over the whole start card. Three ways that keep it inside the card. Every frame is already picking; press faces to try them.

A · The row becomes yours

Press your seat and the row of seats turns into your animals, four at a time between a back and a next arrow, with dots for where you are. The card does not change size at all. Press your seat again to go back to everyone.

B · Opens inside the card

Press your seat and a tray of all your animals opens inside the card, just under the row, eight across. The card grows a little taller and nothing is covered. Done (or picking one) closes it.

C · Your seat steps through them

No list at all. Each press of your seat puts your next animal on it, with a small count (3/17) on it. The smallest: the card never changes.
