Loop · Where your stuff lives

Three ways to keep your loops and intents one tap away

Today a dropped intent only exists on the Floor, then hides under You → Intents. Commitment apps (Partiful, Luma, Airbnb Trips) put yours on the first screen and let discovery sit beside it. Each phone is clickable: switch chips, join a row, tap ＋ to drop an intent and watch where it lands.

A Partiful row

Chips over one list

Home is one scrollable list with a Partiful-style category row: 🔍 · Upcoming · Waiting · Near you · Past, counts on each. 🔍 opens the intent composer full-height, and its result is the Floor. You tab becomes profile and safety only.

try: tap 🔍 → Drop it · tap Join on a Near you row
B Luma / Meetup

“Your week” pinned above the feed

The discovery feed stays as is. A pinned block at the top lists your running loops and waiting intents together, tappable, with a “See all” into You. Least change, one glance answers “what did I sign up for?”

try: tap ＋ to drop an intent · watch the block
C Airbnb Trips

“Mine” as its own tab

Bottom bar gains a Mine tab: Upcoming · Waiting · Past, defaulting to Upcoming. Dropping an intent lands you on Mine → Waiting so you see it exist. Home stays pure discovery; profile stays in the avatar.

try: switch Home / Mine · tap ＋ from Home
