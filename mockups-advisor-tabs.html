Sub tabs: three ways to split her screen

One screen was too much. Each tab below is a whole screen with its own headline, so it can breathe. The split is the decision: whose students, what she does, or which question. Each phone opens on the tab that makes it different; tap the others.

A · whose students

Advisees · Campus

Two tabs. Hers, and everyone else's. The Campus tab is the whole rollup with cold rows actionable inline, so it never needs a third tab. Fewest decisions for her; the campus tab is the long one.

try: tap Campus · tap Jameson
B · what she does

Check on · Route · Pulse

Three verbs. Check on is her caseload. Route is only the spots with someone to send them to, plus the ones slipping. Pulse is numbers, no actions: the trend and every house, course and year. Actions and reading never share a tab.

try: send a nudge · tap Pulse · tap Course
C · which question

How are we · Where's it cold · Who's mine

Three questions, in the order she'd ask them. How are we is a short brief: the number, the trend, highs and lows. Where's it cold is the ranked map with the why and the action folded under each row. Who's mine is her advisees.

try: tap How are we · tap ECON 1110
